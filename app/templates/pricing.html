What changes the price?

  • Quantity: setup costs are shared across the run, so price per box falls as you order more.
  • Size: bigger boxes use more board and cost more to ship.
  • Material: white and thicker B-flute board cost more than kraft E-flute.
  • Printing: printing inside adds about a third; plain boxes cost less.
  • Finishes: foil, spot UV and embossing are quoted separately.
