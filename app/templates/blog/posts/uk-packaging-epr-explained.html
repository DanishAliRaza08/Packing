UK packaging Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) makes businesses that put packaging on the UK market pay for and report on it. Here's what it means for a small brand, in plain words.

This is general information, not legal advice. Rules change, so check the latest guidance on GOV.UK or with an adviser.

Who it applies to

  • UK-established businesses. The UK importer or brand owner usually carries the duty, not an overseas supplier.
  • Thresholds: businesses with more than £1 million turnover that handle over 25 tonnes of packaging a year must register and report.
  • Small producers (between £1 million and £2 million turnover, or 25–50 tonnes) register and report data but don't pay waste management fees.
  • Large producers pay fees that depend on how recyclable their packaging is.

What it means for your boxes

Even if you're under the thresholds today, choosing easy-to-recycle packaging now saves work later, and customers increasingly care about it.

  • Plain corrugated or card boxes with water-based inks
  • No plastic windows or plastic lamination
  • Paper tape and card inserts instead of plastic and foam
  • The material type and weight per box recorded on every invoice
